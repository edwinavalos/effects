Chord practice

You're playing–

Hover a chord's tab and click it to pick a different fingering; the choice is remembered for this browser session. Click a chord name to jump to it. With Listen on, strum the chord: it turns green and moves on when it hears the right one. If the next chord is the same one, let the strings ring out or mute them first. Shapes assume standard tuning. Your effects stay on while you practise.

Rhythm practice

Press Start: you get a one-bar count-in, then play along with the click. Green is on the beat (within 40 ms), yellow close, orange off, red missed. In scale mode every hit is the next note of the scale, so you practise the scale while keeping the rhythm; a note in the wrong pitch turns purple. The arrows show the picking or strumming direction, but only timing is judged, not whether you went down or up. Use headphones so the click isn't picked up by your input.
